Delete Your Account

Pizzawiz

You can delete your Pizzawiz account and all of its data at any time. There are two ways to do this, depending on whether you still have the app installed.

Option 1: Delete in the app (instant)

  1. Open Pizzawiz and sign in
  2. Go to the Profile tab
  3. Under Account, tap Delete My Account
  4. Type DELETE to confirm, then tap the delete button

This permanently and immediately deletes your account. There's no waiting period and no way to undo it.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed, email us at kevinluca65@gmail.com from the address associated with your account and ask us to delete it. We'll process the request within a few business days and confirm by email once it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile (nickname, username, avatar)
  • All your check-ins and passport stamps
  • All photos you've uploaded
  • Your public visits, achievement badges, and blocked-user list
  • Your account itself — you'd need to sign up again to use Pizzawiz afterward

For the full picture of what we collect and why, see the Privacy Policy.
