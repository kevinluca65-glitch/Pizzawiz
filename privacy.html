Pizzawiz Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 19, 2026

Pizzawiz ("Pizzawiz", "we", "us") is a mobile app for tracking the pizzerias you visit, collecting passport stamps, and sharing your pizza journey. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

  • Account information. If you create an account, we collect your email address, or basic profile information (such as your name) if you sign in with Google or Apple. Creating an account is optional — the app is fully usable without one, in which case your data stays only on your device.
  • Profile information you provide. Nickname, home region, favorite pizza style, and a profile photo, if you choose to add them.
  • Location. With your permission, we use your device's location to show nearby pizzerias on the map and to verify that a check-in happened at the pizzeria's real location. Location is only accessed while you're using the app for these features.
  • Photos. Photos you choose to attach to a check-in, taken with your camera or selected from your photo library. We only access photos you explicitly choose to share.
  • User content. Check-in notes, ratings, and any corrections you submit through "Suggest an Edit" for a pizzeria's information.

We do not use advertising or analytics SDKs at this time, so we do not collect advertising identifiers or behavioral tracking data.

How We Use Your Information

  • To operate core features: check-ins, your passport, and stats
  • To verify check-ins by comparing your location to the pizzeria's address
  • To sync your data across your devices when you're signed in
  • To improve the accuracy of pizzeria information, using edit suggestions
  • To generate the shareable image when you choose to share a check-in

Data Storage & Third-Party Services

Account data, check-ins, and profile information are stored using Supabase, our backend and database provider. If you sign in with Google or Apple, those companies process your sign-in according to their own privacy policies. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it with third parties for advertising purposes.

Data Retention & Deletion

If you use the app without an account, your data is stored only on your device and is removed if you uninstall the app. If you create an account, your data is retained until you ask us to delete it. To request deletion of your account and associated data, contact us at the email below — we'll process the request promptly.

Children's Privacy

Pizzawiz is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information. Contact us using the details below to exercise these rights.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We'll post any updates on this page with a new effective date.
